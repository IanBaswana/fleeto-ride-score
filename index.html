    How the score works

    Each behaviour gets its own score from 0 to 100. The ride score is the weighted average. All limits and weights are placeholders until we calibrate them with real rides.

      Before you ride

      • Use Chrome on Android, or Safari on iPhone. Allow location and motion when asked.
      • Keep this screen open during the ride. The app asks the phone to keep the screen on. If the screen locks or you switch apps, the browser stops sending GPS.
      • Mount the phone firmly. A loose phone looks like phone use.
      • Do not touch the phone while you ride. Stop first, then tap Stop ride.
